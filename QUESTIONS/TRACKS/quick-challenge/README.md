# Quick Challenge

Marketing R Us (MRU) is a fictional marketing agency whose teams use AI to plan campaigns, create content, prepare data, and summarize results. Management wants to understand whether the time gained justifies the AI fees and the effort spent reviewing and correcting the work.

Explore Campaigns 1 and 2 from September 2026 and answer the six questions below. You can complete this exercise in Excel and choose how to present your findings.

## About the Data

Each record describes a completed task, including its AI usage and staff hours. Baseline hours estimate the time needed without AI. Actual hours cover all work performed with AI, including review and rework. Prices are in US dollars per million tokens. Use **$50 per hour** to value staff time. All data and prices are fictional.

## Questions

1. How many input and output tokens did each campaign use, what did they cost, and how many prompts were made?
2. How many hours did each campaign save or lose against its no AI estimate?
3. After paying for AI, was estimated net value positive or negative for each campaign?
4. Which department had the highest AI spending, and which had the highest net AI value?
5. Which campaign had more rework, and which recorded issues appeared most often?
6. Should MRU keep, change, or reduce how it uses AI? Give two actions supported by the numbers.
