# Quick Challenge submission checklist

## Submit these items

- One `.xlsx` workbook with Raw and Clean data, visible calculation columns, PivotTables, and one Dashboard sheet.
- One screenshot or PDF of the dashboard with readable labels, units, and totals.
- A **150 to 250 word recommendation** answering whether MRU should keep, change, or reduce AI use, with two actions supported by your numbers.
- A brief disclosure of outside AI help, including tool, approximate minutes, and topic, or a statement that you used none.

Basic BI users may submit an equivalent dashboard file or accessible view, plus their cleaned CSV and a calculation summary. Ensure the reviewer can access the work without private credentials.

## Dashboard requirements

- KPI cells for calculated AI spend, total tokens, total prompts, net hours saved, and net AI value.
- A comparison of both campaigns, including input and output tokens, cost, hours, net value, and aggregate ROI.
- A department AI spending chart.
- A baseline versus actual hours chart.
- Clear USD and hours units, informative titles, and visible negative values.

Product breakdowns and rework charts are optional. Answer the rework question in a summary table or your written analysis if you do not chart it.

## Review before submitting

- Confirm 120 unique completed tasks after removing exact repeated task records.
- Confirm all departments use one of the four standard labels.
- Preserve the original raw CSV and explain your cleaning steps.
- Confirm input tokens, output tokens, and prompts are nonnegative whole numbers; rework hours do not exceed actual hours.
- Calculate costs from the two supplied rates and retain precision until display.
- Count actual hours once and do not add rework to actual hours.
- Calculate summary ROI and average prompt cost from totals, with N/A for a zero denominator.
- Confirm PivotTables, dashboard KPI cells, and cleaned-data totals agree after refreshing.
- Explain that time value estimates staff capacity, not cash payroll savings or causal proof.
- Answer all six questions and support two recommended actions with numbers.
- If using a date chart, label it spending by task completion date.
- Keep the final recommendation between 150 and 250 words.

## Recommendation outline

Describe the business decision in one sentence. Compare the campaigns using your cost, time, and net-value results. Name the department or workflow observation that matters most. Propose two specific actions, with numerical evidence for each. End with a limitation or a measurement MRU should collect next. Write this yourself; there is no supplied conclusion to copy.
